SysMonitor icon
===============

The SysMonitor application icon is based on the "activity" icon from Lucide.

Source: https://lucide.dev/
Icon source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/activity.svg
License: ISC License
Copyright (c) Lucide Icons and Contributors

The generated Windows .ico adds a custom blue rounded-square background for
better readability at taskbar and tray sizes.
